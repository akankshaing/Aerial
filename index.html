Gesture guide

☝️
DrawPoint with your index finger to paint. Pinch thumb + index to change stroke width on the fly.
✌️
MoveIndex + middle up lifts the pen — reposition without drawing.
🖐️
EraseOpen palm, all five fingers up, wipes ink near your hand.
✊
Clear allMake a fist and hold for about a second to wipe the board.
🤙
Cycle colorThumb + pinky extended switches to the next brush color.
